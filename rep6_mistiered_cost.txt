REP 6 — The cost of a mis-tiered action

From sample_incident.log:

node-7 (safe): PROPOSE remediate=rotate-logs autonomy=approve
  reversible=false -- GATE waiting for human approval -- 8 minutes
  later, oncall (m.litman) approves with token=I-APPROVE -- THEN the
  agent acts.

node-4 (expensive): PROPOSE remediate=scale-volume
  autonomy=ACT(misconfigured) -- the agent acts on the SAME line it
  proposed, with no gate, no wait, no human -- immediately followed by
  a FinOps alert for +$1840/mo in unplanned spend with no approval on
  record.

The single configuration difference: the autonomy TIER assigned to the
proposed remediation. node-7's action was correctly configured as
approve; node-4's action was misconfigured as ACT. Nothing about the
diagnosis, the alert, or the underlying disk-pressure signal was
different or wrong between the two cases -- both were legitimate,
correctly-diagnosed incidents. The entire difference between an 8-minute
gated incident that closed cleanly and an unapproved, ongoing
$1840/month cost commitment was one YAML field's value.
